# Elomental brand voice

Elomental talks like a sharp friend across the board: someone who plays well,
enjoys a clever line, and is glad you're here, whether you're chasing Elo or
just unwinding with a square before bed.

This guide covers every word a player can see or hear: labels, buttons,
messages, hints, results, errors, share texts, and what screen readers say.

---

## 1. Voice pillars

### Encouraging
Progress is celebrated, effort is noticed, and being stuck is part of the game.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| "Two mistakes, still standing. Solved." | "You made 2 mistakes." |
| "Stuck is where the good moves hide. Try a hint." | "No moves found." |
| "Not solved yet. The day counts once you solve it." | "Failed." |

### A bit funny
Light wit about logic, numbers, grids and strategy; a nod to chess is welcome.
One smile per moment at most, and never when something went wrong for the player.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| "Checkmate, grid." (a clean win) | "OMG you absolutely DESTROYED that puzzle 🔥🔥" |
| "Forging your square…" | "Hold tight, our hamsters are generating your puzzle lol" |

### Respectful of intelligence
Short, confident, specific. No baby talk, no filler, at most one exclamation
mark in a message.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| "Speed bonus +15%" | "Wow, you were super speedy! Great job!!" |
| "3 hints per game. Each one trims your Stardust." | "Remember, you only get a few hints, so use them wisely, okay?" |

### Fair to both players at the table
Competitive players get the numbers straight (time, Elo, penalties). Casual
players get reassurance and no pressure. The same sentence should work for both.

| We are | We are not |
| --- | --- |
| "No Elo lost: your rating is protected below 500." | "Don't worry, losing is okay!" |
| "No pressure, no Elo." | "Casual mode for noobs" |

## 3. Humor rules

**Allowed:** wins, loading lines, Clash invitations, empty states that aren't
errors, the welcome. Wordplay on logic, grids, numbers, openings, endgames,
checkmate, forks, gambits.

**Banned:** errors, lost progress, connection problems, sign-in and account
issues, bans and moderation, anything about money, losses and concedes, and
anything a screen reader says (aria-labels and alt text are literal).

**Calibration**

| Too much | Just right | Too dry |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "BOOM! The grid never stood a chance! 🎉🎉" | "Checkmate, grid." | "Puzzle solved." |
| "Our elves are carving your sudoku out of pure logic…" | "Forging your square…" | "Loading…" |
| "Mistakes? Never heard of her." | "Clean solve: no mistakes, no hints." | "Mistakes: 0. Hints: 0." |
| "Uh-oh, someone's got butterfingers!" (on a mistake) | "Wrong number. Two mistakes left." | "Error." |

One joke per message, and the message must still work if the joke doesn't land.

## 5. Style rules

**Capitalisation.** Sentence case everywhere in source text ("Clash log",
"Play square"); the design uppercases headings with CSS. Proper names keep
their capitals: Elomental, Casual, Ranked, Daily Square, Clash, Elo, Stardust,
Rusted Crown, Setup, Review.

**Punctuation.**
- Full sentences end with a period; labels, buttons, titles and stat values don't.
- Typographic quotes and apostrophes: ’ “ ”. Ellipsis is one character: …
- No em dashes in player copy: use a colon or a new sentence.
- At most one exclamation mark per message, and only for celebrations.
- Two short sentences beat one long one.

**Emoji.** None. The app's icons carry that job.

**Numbers and time.**
- Numerals always: "3 hints", "2 mistakes left", "1 pause left".
- Time as m:ss ("3:07"); waits as "2h 15m".
- Signed changes: "+12 Stardust", "Elo 1240 → 1262 (+22)", "Speed bonus +15%".
- Fractions as "12 of 31" in sentences, "12 / 31" in stat rows.

**Buttons.** One to three words, starting with a verb where possible: "Play
square", "Copy", "Try again", "Keep playing". The primary action names what
happens, never "OK" or "Submit".

**Person.** Talk to the player as "you". The app doesn't say "we" except in
sign-in emails ("We sent a link…"). Never "the user".

**Inclusive and accessible.**
- Gender-neutral ("your friend", "they").
- No idioms that rely on sight for screen-reader text.
- aria-labels and alt text say literally what the control is or does: "Row 3,
  column 5, empty", "Close", "Copy square code".
- No meaning by colour alone in text ("the red cells" → "the cells with mistakes").
- Avoid ableist or violent slang ("crazy", "kill it", "insane").

**Length.** Toasts fit in two lines on a 375px phone (about 70 characters).
Buttons fit their tile; check any new label at 375px.

## 7. Pre-ship checklist for new text

1. Uses the glossary term (cell vs square, Elo, Stardust, game, mistake, hint).
2. Sentence case; periods only on full sentences; ’ “ ” …; no em dashes.
3. At most one exclamation mark, only if it celebrates.
4. No humor in errors, losses, sign-in, account, money or moderation.
5. Says what happened and what to do next, in that order.
6. Fits a 375px screen (toasts ≤ about 70 characters, buttons ≤ 3 words).
7. Lives in the copy module, never hardcoded in a screen.
8. aria-labels and alt text are literal and complete.
9. Reads fine to both a 2000-Elo grinder and a first-time player.
